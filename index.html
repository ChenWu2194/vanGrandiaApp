Fees & fuel assumptions

“×” = how much more fuel is used than in free flow (severe = 2× ≈ 4.5 km/L). Every booking gets its fees added automatically: van rental (a daily rate plus an extra amount for each block of fuel cost), the driver fee per day, and the affiliate extra per day when a booking is marked as affiliate. Fuel is always rounded up to the next ₱100.
